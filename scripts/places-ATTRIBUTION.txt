The places index (built by scripts/build-places.mjs into .cache/places/,
served from R2 under places/v1/) comes from

1. GeoNames: cities500 (the world, places over 500 people) and, for ES FR PT
   IT AD BE NL LU CH DE GB IE, every populated place of the per-country dumps,
   each with its IANA time zone.
   https://www.geonames.org · https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
   Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0):
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Changes: only the name, the Latin-script alternate names (normalised),
   the country, the population and the time zone are kept, split into files
   by the first letters of the names.

2. airportsdata, airports with an IATA code (code, name, city, country, time zone).
   https://github.com/mborsetti/airportsdata
